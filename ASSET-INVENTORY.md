# Supplied asset inventory

Visual review covered 49 supplied JPEG photographs (1280px-class originals, approximately 60–176 KB each). A contact sheet was inspected to distinguish staff portraits, ward and care images, laboratory photos, outreach and exterior views. The official `Hospital Logo.png` (1614×975) is the website identity source. An optimized 600×362 derivative is used in navigation and footers; a square 64×64 favicon derivative preserves the full logo on white without cropping. The original remains unchanged. All 20 selected site photographs are optimized WebP files in `assets/images/` (about 1.9 MB combined). Every published derivative is referenced by at least one page; repeated references are limited to photos that meaningfully serve more than one section.

## Official brand asset

- `Hospital Logo.png` — inspected at 1614×975; preserved as supplied and used to create `assets/hospital-logo.png` (600×362, 120 KB) and `assets/favicon.png` (64×64). The derivative keeps the complete aspect ratio and white background; no logo elements or colours were changed.

Selected photographs were copied from the supplied originals into `assets/images/` as WebP. The website references these publishable derivatives; the source `pictures/` directory is not required for deployment.
