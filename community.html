Health beyond our walls

Stronger communities start with better health.

Gifted Minds Medical Centre describes an active role in health promotion and community outreach across Kisumu County.

Community health promotion

Health information and care, closer to community.

The facility profile describes medical camps and screening as part of the centre’s community health promotion activity. These efforts address health awareness and support people to seek care.

Free medical campsHypertension and diabetes screeningMaternal and child health awarenessSchool health educationImmunisation drivesPublic health education forums

Working with local structures

Community connections matter.

Community initiatives are supported through relationships with local groups and institutions, as described in the centre’s profile.

Community Health Volunteers · Local schools and institutions · Faith-based organisations · Non-governmental organisations · Local community leaders

Social responsibility

Support for community wellbeing.

The profile lists corporate social responsibility activities including free treatment for vulnerable patients, subsidised maternal services, sanitation and hygiene awareness, public health education forums and youth health mentorship.

For information about current community programmes, contact the centre directly.
