Coverage information

Clear information about healthcare cover.

The facility profile distinguishes current accreditation from insurance and corporate arrangements that are still being pursued. Please confirm coverage for your specific plan with the centre and your insurer.

Status in the facility profile

What is accredited, and what is in progress.

Gifted Minds Medical Centre lists SHIF and Birdview Insurance as accredited. It describes private health insurance providers and corporate health schemes as partnerships being pursued.

Insurance eligibility and benefits may depend on the terms of your plan. Contact the centre before your visit if you need to check an arrangement.
