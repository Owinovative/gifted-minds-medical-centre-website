# Healthcare website design research

Research reviewed on 12 November 2026. This is a design synthesis, not a source for Gifted Minds' institutional claims.

## Useful patterns observed

### Patient tasks lead the navigation

Across the reviewed sites, high-use destinations are easy to find: care and services, clinicians, locations, patient information, insurance and contact. Several homepages also surface a small set of task-oriented actions before deeper editorial content. The pattern is useful because patients arrive with practical needs as well as a need to understand the institution.

### Homepage and detail pages have different jobs

Homepages introduce the institution and route visitors to more specific pages. Service, provider, patient and location pages then give each topic a clearer hierarchy and a more focused next action. Breadcrumbs and consistent global navigation help users retain their place as they move between those pages.

### Trust is supported by useful detail

Strong institutional pages make practical information findable: what a service includes, how to prepare for care, how to locate a facility, or how insurance is handled. Established institutions also display awards and outcome claims; those patterns only work because the evidence belongs to those institutions. Gifted Minds' pages therefore use verifiable profile information and do not import benchmark claims.

### Photography, editorial content and utility work together

The reviewed sites combine strong photography with short explanatory copy, service groupings, and clear patient actions. Their footers repeat important navigation and contact routes. This creates visual confidence without making every page a wall of decorative cards.

### Scale-appropriate navigation matters

Large systems use search, provider directories, portals, location finders and deep service taxonomies. These are supported by large operational systems. Gifted Minds is an independent centre, so its information architecture uses direct page links, verified service categories, telephone, email, WhatsApp and directions instead of simulating unavailable digital services.

## Adaptation for Gifted Minds Medical Centre

- Use a genuine image of the centre and its team to introduce the institution, rather than generic hospital stock photography.
- Keep the primary navigation compact and route to separate About, Services, Team, Facilities, Insurance, Community and Contact pages.
- Make immediate patient tasks available consistently: call, WhatsApp, email and directions.
- Give each page a distinct composition and a clear next step while keeping shared navigation, footer and visual tokens consistent.
- Organise the service page around the actual profile: outpatient, inpatient, minor procedures, laboratory, pharmacy, specialised care and maternal and child health.
- Present insurance accurately: SHIF and Birdview are described as accredited in the supplied profile; private health insurers and corporate schemes are described as being pursued.
- Use real supplied photos for the centre frontage, ward, laboratory, care interactions, staff and community. Use factual captions and meaningful alternative text.
- Do not add an appointment portal, provider search, online patient account, award claims or service lines that are not documented as currently available.

## Proposed visual identity

The supplied Gifted Minds emblem is the authority for the identity: its green cross, blue stethoscope and red pulse remain intact in the header, footer and favicon. A proportion-preserving derivative keeps the entire mark visible. The rest of the site pairs that green with midnight navy, warm white, pale stone and restrained brass-gold details; red remains part of the mark rather than a broad interface colour. Manrope headings and DM Sans body text create a compact, readable hierarchy. Full-width section backgrounds, fluid grids, large real photographs and a 96% fluid desktop content canvas reduce the narrow-centred-page effect while keeping text line lengths controlled. The identity is original and does not reproduce any reference site's visual assets, copy or distinctive components.
